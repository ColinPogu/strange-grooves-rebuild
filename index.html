About the Show

Life's Deep Cuts, One Conversation at a Time

Strange Grooves explores places in rock 'n' roll history and chats with guests, musicians, and people from all walks of life. It is a show about the music we love, the lives behind it, and what keeps us all inspired.

From vinyl collectors to touring bands to local legends, every episode digs a little deeper into the records and the stories that stay with us.
